Search "content creation course India" and you get ads, not answers: everyone is selling a course and nobody says what the others cost. So this compares the real options by what you actually pay for. Information about the platforms is free and always has been. What costs money is a person reviewing your posts, and the price bands on this page are mostly a measure of how much of that you get.

We run two of the programmes on this list. They are included on the same terms as the rest, including where they are the wrong choice.

## Free: the platforms themselves

YouTube and Meta both publish free courses on their own products. YouTube's Creator Academy covers channel setup, audience building, analytics and monetisation across beginner to advanced tiers. Meta Blueprint's Instagram courses cover the tools and, mostly, the ads.

**Do these first.** They are free, they are correct about the platform's own rules, and if you cannot finish a free course you will not finish a paid one.

**Do not stop here if** you need a reason to post every week. Nothing free provides one.

## Recorded courses from creators

The best value in the whole table, and the one most people should buy before anything else.

Ankur Warikoo's How To YouTube, on WebVeda, is 22 hours across eleven modules, ₹1,999 once for lifetime access or the same per year, with a 14-day refund window. It covers audience, content systems, editing, analytics, monetisation and AI tools, and the page showed over 35,000 enrolled. His How To Instagram is ₹1,449 to ₹1,749 depending on tier; the top tier adds six live Q&A sessions. BeerBiceps' Instagram 101 is ₹1,299, about ten hours, in Hinglish, lifetime access. His Podcasting 101 is ₹4,999 for six hours.

**Pick a recorded course if** you are starting, you want the mechanics explained by someone who has done it, and you are prepared to hold yourself to a schedule.

**Do not pick one if** you have already made thirty posts and cannot work out why they do not land. A recording cannot tell you. That is the point at which you pay for a person.

## Live cohorts

The category we are in. This is where the price jumps, and the honest reason is labour: someone is reading your posts.

SourceKode's Content Creator Program is ₹15,000, shown as a discount from ₹25,000, over 14 weeks, live online in batches of 25 with a personal mentor and a seven-day money-back guarantee. The Indian Institute of Creators runs a one-month Full Digital Creator Mastery at ₹49,900, labelled introductory against ₹99,900, through its app with on-ground sessions, limited to 30. Morph Academy in Chandigarh runs a three-month hybrid course for ₹45,000 with a physical classroom, which for people in the north is the reason to choose it.

LevelUp's Creator Academy is twelve weeks of live weekend classes at ₹45,000, with no-cost EMI. The specific thing it sells is that every post you make during the course is reviewed by a mentor before it goes out, plus a 21-day posting sprint on your real account and a pod of five or six other creators. It is aimed at founders, working professionals and marketers, not at teenagers. The page does not state the next cohort date, the batch size or the refund terms, which is a weakness of our own page, and we say so here.

**Pick a live cohort if** you are already posting, you have a job or a business that the content serves, and the thing you lack is someone telling you which of your posts are bad and why.

**Do not pick one if** you have not yet made anything. Spend ₹1,999 first. Nobody can review posts that do not exist.

## Residencies

The Forge Creator Bootcamp is the in-person version: 24 creators, mentors on site, and a body of content made in one place. It runs 12 days, part online and part in person, for ₹85,000.

**Pick a residency if** you can take a week off, you learn by doing rather than by watching, and you want thirty pieces of content made under supervision rather than one lecture about how to make them.

**Do not pick one if** ₹85,000 is a stretch. A residency does not teach anything a cohort does not. It compresses it.

## So which one

Answer one question first: **have you already posted enough to be stuck?**

If no, do the free platform courses, then buy one recorded course for under ₹2,000, and post thirty times. Most people never get past this stage, and no course in the ₹45,000 band will change that. If yes, and you have a business or a career the content is meant to serve, a live cohort is the first thing on this page that reviews your actual work, and that is what you are paying for. If yes and you want to do a year's worth of making in a week, a residency.

Whatever you choose, ask one question before paying anything above ₹2,000: **who reads my posts, and how often?** If the answer is nobody, you are buying a recording at a cohort's price.

## Common questions

**Is it live or recorded, and how long do I keep access?**
Warikoo's courses are recorded with lifetime access on the one-time price. BeerBiceps' Instagram and podcasting courses are recorded, lifetime. SourceKode, Morph and LevelUp are live on a schedule. Check the access period on anything recorded: BeerBiceps' YouTube course, for instance, expires after a year.

**What is the refund policy?**
Warikoo's courses give 14 days. SourceKode gives seven. Growth School states it gives none. LevelUp and the Forge link to a refund policy rather than stating it on the course page, which we should fix.

**Is the price I see the real price?**
Often not. The Indian Institute of Creators, SourceKode, upGrad and Growth School all show a struck-through higher figure next to the one you pay. upGrad's AI content-creation course showed two different prices, ₹19,999 and ₹39,999, on the same page. Take a screenshot of the price on the day.

**Do I need a course at all?**
To learn the platforms, no. To post consistently and improve, most people need either a deadline or a reader, and a course is one way to buy both. It is not the only way.

**Can a course get me sponsors?**
No course on this page claims that, and you should walk away from any that does. Sponsors follow an audience. The course, at best, helps you build one faster.
