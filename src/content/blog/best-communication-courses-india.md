Search "executive presence course India" and the results are business-school brochures at one end and public-speaking coaches with no prices at the other. Neither tells you the one thing that decides it: how often you will actually stand up and speak, and who will tell you what went wrong. So this compares the options on fee, hours, and practice, with the source for every figure at the foot of the page.

We run one of the programmes on this list. It is included on the same terms as the rest, including where it is the wrong choice.

## The business schools

ISB, IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore and IIM Calcutta all sell executive presence or leadership communication, and the pattern is the same: short, senior, expensive, and aimed at people whose company is paying.

ISB's Mastering Executive Presence is 15 weeks, live online with self-paced material, ₹1,18,000 plus taxes, with financing available, and runs from 19 September 2026 to 3 January 2027. Its Business Storytelling and Executive Presence is three days on campus for ₹1,25,000 plus taxes, 27 to 29 September 2026, and asks for five years of post-degree experience. IIM Ahmedabad's The Winning Edge is six days, 16 to 21 November 2026, at ₹1,91,250 plus 18% GST, or ₹1,77,863 if you apply by 26 October, for people heading a division or a department. IIM Bangalore's three-day Executive Presence for Impactful Leadership, 25 to 27 November 2026 on campus, is ₹90,000 non-residential or ₹1,06,500 residential, before GST, with early-bird discounts of about 10% until 4 November. IIM Calcutta's Advanced Programme in Strategic Leadership Communication is the long one: six months of Saturday-morning classes online plus two campus visits, ₹2,80,000 plus GST, for senior managers with five years' experience, starting November 2026. Its recorded Mastering Storytelling course is 20 weeks at ₹1,64,900 plus tax. IIM Kozhikode runs a 12-week online version through Emeritus; the current fee is not on the page, and the previous batch's brochure listed ₹1,10,000 plus GST.

None of these pages states how many people are in the room.

**Pick a business school if** your employer is paying, you are senior enough that the peers in the room matter as much as the teaching, and the certificate has value in your organisation.

**Do not pick one if** you are paying yourself and what you need is repetition. Three days on a campus is three days. Presence is a habit, and habits are built weekly.

## Toastmasters

Not a course. A club. You join, you turn up weekly, you give prepared speeches and get evaluated by other members, and over a year you speak in front of people forty times. International dues are $72 every six months plus a $25 joining fee, paid in dollars, with 18% GST for members in India; District 224's own calculator puts the first year at about ₹17,343 including club dues, and calls that figure indicative.

**Pick Toastmasters if** you are early in this, you need a room every week, and you can build your own curriculum from the evaluations. For the money, nothing on this page comes close.

**Do not pick it if** you want a coach. Evaluations come from peers, and the standard of feedback is whatever your local club's standard is.

## Recorded courses

Coursera Plus is ₹2,099 a month, or ₹7,499 a year on the promotion running when we checked, and includes courses like How to Build an Executive Presence, which takes about two hours. LinkedIn Learning's India price is behind a login, so it is not stated here. IIM Calcutta's recorded storytelling course is the business-school version of the same thing at ₹1,64,900.

**Pick a recorded course if** you want the vocabulary and the frameworks. That is what these give you, cheaply.

**Do not pick one as** your training. Nobody watches you speak. Presence is the one skill that cannot be learnt from a recording, because the recording cannot see you.

## Live cohorts with practice groups

The category we are in. LevelUp's Executive Presence Cohort is twelve weeks at ₹55,000, all-inclusive, with no-cost EMI. Live sessions with specialists run on Saturday and Sunday, and twice a week, Tuesday and Thursday, you meet for 45 minutes with a practice group of four. The page says to budget about five hours a week around a full-time job. Forty seats, ten groups of four, a certificate on finishing all twelve weeks. It is aimed at senior specialists, team leads, founders and leaders in global teams with around five years' experience and real rooms to practise on. The next cohort starts 24 October 2026. Our page does not say whether ₹55,000 includes GST; it should, and we will fix that.

Dale Carnegie India runs the well-known Dale Carnegie Course and High Impact Presentations, live online and in person, but publishes no fee that we could find, so it is not in the table.

**Pick a live cohort if** you have a job, you are past the point where a book helps, and what you need is to speak twice a week with someone experienced telling you what to change.

**Do not pick one if** what you actually need is a certificate from a name your board recognises. That is what the business schools sell, and they sell it honestly.

## So which one

Answer one question first: **how many times a week will this make you speak?**

Recorded courses: zero. Business-school short programmes: many times, for three to six days, then nothing. Toastmasters: once a week, for as long as you turn up, with peer feedback. A live cohort with practice groups: two or three times a week for twelve weeks, with a mentor. IIM Calcutta's six-month programme: weekly, at five times the price.

If your company pays and the peers matter, a business school. If you are paying and you are early, Toastmasters, and take it seriously. If you are paying, you are mid-career, and you need the repetition with someone qualified watching, a cohort. Whichever you pick, the theory is available for ₹2,099 a month on Coursera, and you should read it before any of them.

## Common questions

**Do the fees include GST?**
At the business schools, no: every page says "plus taxes" or "plus 18% GST", and IIM Bangalore prices residential and non-residential separately. Toastmasters adds 18% GST for India. Our page says all-inclusive and should say whether that covers GST.

**How many hours a week?**
LevelUp's page says about five. IIM Kozhikode says four to six. IIM Calcutta's long programme is three hours every Saturday morning plus campus weeks. The three-day programmes are full days.

**Will I get a certificate, and from whom?**
ISB, the IIMs and LevelUp all certify completion. IIM Kozhikode requires 70% to certify. Toastmasters has its own education path rather than a course certificate. Coursera certifies each course.

**What is the minimum experience?**
ISB's storytelling programme and IIM Calcutta's long programme both ask for five years. LevelUp's page describes around five. IIM Ahmedabad's programme is for people heading a division. Toastmasters and Coursera have none.

**Can my employer nominate me?**
The business schools are built for it, and IIM Kozhikode offers up to 10% off for corporate plans. Ask any provider; most will invoice a company.
