## 9. The 12 weeks (curriculum, the centerpiece)
- **Eyebrow:** THE CURRICULUM
- **Headline:** Twelve weeks. Three builds. Zero fluff.
- **Subhead:** Exactly what you learn, exactly what you use, and exactly what ships, week by week.

**Module 00 · Foundations — Weeks 1 to 2: Become AI-native.**
- Learn: the model toolkit and when to use which; prompt and context engineering as a repeatable method; custom assistants trained on your own data.
- Use: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, custom GPTs.
- Ship: your AI command center and two custom assistants.

**Module 01 · Content — Weeks 3 to 5: Build your content engine.**
- Learn: turning ideas into a repeatable content system; training brand voice; multi-format generation; review and scheduling.
- Use: ChatGPT, Claude, your custom assistants, a scheduling and publishing stack.
- Ship: a content engine producing on-brand content for your brand every week.

**Module 02 · Operations — Weeks 6 to 8: Build a live automation.**
- Learn: how agentic workflows work; triggers and actions; AI enrichment; connecting tools into multi-step agents that run without you.
- Use: n8n, Make, Zapier, and AI agents.
- Ship: a live automation running a real piece of your operation, 24/7.

**Module 03 · Product — Weeks 9 to 11: Build and deploy a product.**
- Learn: AI product thinking; vibe coding; building and shipping a working app with no code; getting it in front of real users.
- Use: Lovable, Cursor, Supabase, Vercel.
- Ship: a real, deployed product or internal tool.

**Module 04 · Demo Day — Week 12: Show your three builds.**
- Present your content engine, automation, and product to mentors and the cohort. Leave with a 12-month plan and a certificate.

- **Secondary CTA:** Download the full curriculum
