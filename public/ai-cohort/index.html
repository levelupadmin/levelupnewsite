The new operator

One AI generalist out-ships an entire team.

An AI generalist does not do the work by hand. They direct AI to do it: research, drafts, analysis, outreach, whole workflows. While most people are still copy-pasting prompts, the generalist has agents running the busywork and spends their time on judgment and strategy. That is the gap this program closes.
